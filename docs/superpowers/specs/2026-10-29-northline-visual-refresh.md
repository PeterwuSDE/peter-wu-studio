# Northline Studio visual refresh

## Intent
Improve the existing multi-page studio website so visitors understand its services quickly and see evidence of design capability. Preserve Northline Studio branding and the existing production origin, contact address, published pricing, maintenance terms, and static hosting model.

## Authorized scope
The user approved a concise homepage leading to service subpages: websites and modernization, business tools, Shopify commerce, SEO, plus ongoing care. They requested review and improvement of the previous agent's rough page split, especially the hero and transitions between sections. This is presentation work; the SEO audit backend is a separate product project.

## Approaches and decision
Considered a minimal typography-only directory and a more expressive studio presentation. The former repeats the current short-intro/card problem. Proceed with the expressive presentation within the approved service structure: warm paper, existing cobalt and lime accents, large editorial typography, and lightweight illustrative interface compositions. This visual choice is the implementer's recommendation, not an independently validated conversion finding.

## Experience
- A concise hero explains the studio, shows an explicitly illustrative interface composition, and offers project-start and service-exploration actions.
- Four service entrances lead to Websites, Business Tools, Shopify, and SEO. Business Tools and Shopify receive their own pages.
- Ongoing Care has a contrasting, compact band and its existing dedicated page.
- Each service page retains a clear introduction, useful scope and deliverables, related services, and a real contact action.
- Existing abstract diagrams remain explanatory illustrations, never fabricated client work or performance evidence.
- Keep mobile layouts, semantic headings, keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and existing links usable.

## Non-goals
No fake testimonials, customers, case-study results, or business metrics. No claim that the planned automatic SEO checker already works. No new database, account system, checkout, or publishing integration. No changes to published prices or maintenance obligations.

## Validation
Run the static export and existing rendered-HTML suite after updating assertions affected by the approved homepage and new routes. Verify new routes appear in exported files, sitemap, desktop/mobile navigation, and metadata. Build and browser checks must be reported accurately if the environment cannot run them.
