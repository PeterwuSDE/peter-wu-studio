# Northline Studio Positioning and Homepage Design

**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Status:** Approved in conversation; ready for implementation planning
**Pricing revision:** 2026-10-26. Essential is starting from $2,500. Signature stays starting from $3,500 until case studies are added. The single Ongoing Care price is replaced by Care (starting from $199/month) and Care Plus (starting from $399/month).

## 1. Purpose

Reposition Northline Studio from a boutique website-design provider into a design-led digital partner for founders and owner-led businesses. The studio should be able to sell the full range of work it can responsibly deliver—websites, commerce, business systems, deployment, and ongoing management—without presenting itself as a generic technical-services marketplace.

The homepage must make three ideas clear:

1. Northline combines strong visual judgment with serious technical execution.
2. Northline can take a business idea or existing operation from design through implementation and deployment.
3. Northline can continue managing and improving the result so the client does not need to handle the digital side personally.

## 2. Intended Audience

The primary audience is founders and owner-led businesses that have a real business or a credible idea but need help turning it into a polished, dependable digital experience.

They may need Northline because they:

- do not have the design or technical skills to execute the work themselves;
- are focused on running and growing the business rather than managing websites and digital tools;
- care about presentation, credibility, and long-term reliability;
- want one accountable partner to handle design, implementation, deployment, and ongoing support;
- are willing to invest in tailored work rather than choosing the lowest-cost template option.

The copy should remain broad enough to appeal to independent professionals, creators, models, influencers, product brands, service businesses, and growing teams without naming those audiences directly or sounding targeted only at small local businesses.

## 3. Positioning

### Primary positioning

> Northline Studio is a design-led digital partner for founders and owner-led businesses. We design, build, launch, and manage the websites, commerce experiences, and digital systems behind their growth.

### Core promise

> You run the business. We design, build, and manage the digital side of it.

### Hero direction

The homepage should lead with an outcome rather than a list of technologies:

> We build the digital side of ambitious businesses.

Supporting copy should explain that Northline works with founders and owner-led businesses across websites, commerce, digital systems, deployment, and ongoing management.

### Tone

Copy must feel:

- design-conscious and editorial;
- calm, precise, and confident;
- premium without being inflated;
- technically credible without relying on jargon;
- selective, but welcoming to a client who only has an early idea.

Avoid language that frames Northline as a low-cost provider, a local-business specialist, a software-development shop, or a catalogue of disconnected technical services.

## 4. Engagement Model

Northline's default commercial model is a project followed by optional ongoing service:

1. **Strategy and Build** — define the goal, direction, scope, experience, and technical solution.
2. **Launch** — complete implementation, quality assurance, deployment, domain configuration, and handover.
3. **Managed Growth** — maintain, update, and improve the delivered system through an ongoing service when appropriate.

Clients may end the relationship after launch. The homepage should nevertheless make ongoing care feel like a natural extension of the work rather than an afterthought.

### Ongoing service levels

- **Care** — starting from $199/month. Hosting, uptime monitoring, security and dependency updates, backups, and up to 1 hour of small content changes per month.
- **Care Plus** — starting from $399/month. Everything in Care, plus up to 3 hours of changes per month, priority response, and a monthly site report.
- **Ongoing Partnership** is a selective, tailored engagement for clients who need continuous design, development, and digital support.

Care and Care Plus are bounded monthly plans. Additional work is billed at $125/hour, unused hours do not roll over, and the minimum term is 3 months. The public copy must not describe the allowance as open-ended or “agreed content support.” Work beyond those hours—new pages, campaigns, and similar improvements—is either billed at the hourly rate or scoped as a separate project or Ongoing Partnership.

Ongoing Partnership is not a fixed public package. The website may present it as limited-availability work, but it must not promise unlimited work, fixed response at all hours, or a public monthly price. Each engagement must define capacity, priorities, response expectations, minimum term, and out-of-scope billing before work begins.

## 5. Public Service Architecture

The existing five-service list will be consolidated into four clear service families.

### 5.1 Websites and Brand Presence

Custom brand websites, portfolio websites, redesigns, CMS-enabled sites, multilingual experiences, and website migrations. The emphasis is on visual identity, clear content structure, responsive implementation, accessibility, performance, and SEO foundations.

### 5.2 Commerce

Shopify stores, store redesigns, storefront experience, product and collection pages, simple payment flows, subscriptions, and related commerce integrations. Northline's primary public offer is design and theme-level commerce implementation; complex headless builds remain selective custom work.

### 5.3 Business Systems

Booking and calendar systems, client intake, forms, payments and deposits, CRM connections, onboarding flows, workflow automation, memberships, content management, lightweight client portals, and tailored multi-tool suites. These capabilities should be described through business outcomes rather than a long list of platforms.

### 5.4 Ongoing Care

Managed hosting, deployment, backups, security and dependency updates, uptime monitoring, and a defined monthly allowance for content changes, published as Care and Care Plus. This service family also contains the understated invitation to discuss an Ongoing Partnership. It does not promise open-ended content support.

## 6. Pricing Presentation

The pricing section should be framed as **Starting Points**, not as a complete menu or a promise that every project fits a predetermined package.

### Public starting prices

- **The Essential Website** — Starting from $2,500. Scope stays a focused site of up to 3 custom-designed pages, with responsive development, foundational SEO and accessibility, and domain and launch configuration.
- **The Signature Website** — Starting from $3,500. This price stays in place until case studies are added; a later increase is expected and is not part of this revision.
- **Care** — Starting from $199/month
- **Care Plus** — Starting from $399/month

Shared terms, shown with the Care tiers: additional work is billed at $125/hour, unused hours do not roll over, and a 3-month minimum term applies.

### Proposal-based services

- **Commerce** — Custom proposal
- **Business Systems** — Custom proposal
- **Ongoing Partnership** — Tailored engagement; limited availability

Commerce and Business Systems must remain proposal-based because their scope changes materially with products, content, integrations, workflows, data migration, and operational requirements.

The existing itemized Optional Enhancements price list should be removed. Copywriting, brand direction, advanced SEO, additional pages, and priority schedules can still be offered, but their scope and price belong in the client proposal. Removing the itemized list supports the premium positioning and avoids making the studio look like an add-on menu.

Third-party fees—including platform subscriptions, paid themes, paid fonts, applications, transaction fees, and external services—must continue to be described as separate from Northline's service fee.

## 7. Homepage Information Architecture

The visual system and overall page length should remain recognizable. The content hierarchy changes as follows.

### 7.1 Header

Keep the fixed header and the existing primary navigation:

- Services
- Pricing
- Process
- About
- Start a project

The header does not need a link to every section. Desktop and mobile menus must expose the same primary destinations.

### 7.2 Hero

Lead with the new positioning and retain the existing premium visual treatment. The primary call to action should remain a project-start action that leads to the contact section.

The hero should communicate design, engineering, deployment, and managed reliability without presenting a list of technologies.

### 7.3 Three engagement paths

Repurpose the current introductory section to help a visitor recognize their need quickly:

- **Build something new.** For a new business, brand, offer, website, store, or system.
- **Improve what already exists.** For an existing digital presence that needs stronger design, performance, structure, or functionality.
- **Let us manage it for you.** For an owner who wants dependable ongoing operation and improvement without handling every digital detail.

This section replaces the current website-only explanation of what a premium site should do.

### 7.4 Services

Render the four approved service families. Each service should have a short outcome-led description and a small set of representative capabilities. The capabilities are examples, not contractual inclusion lists.

### 7.5 Pricing

Present The Essential Website, The Signature Website, Commerce, and Business Systems as starting points. Ongoing Care should remain a distinct support area with two public monthly tiers, Care and Care Plus, plus the shared hourly, rollover, and minimum-term terms. Ongoing Partnership should appear as a restrained, limited-availability invitation rather than another standardized pricing card.

### 7.6 Process

Use four stages:

1. **Direction** — clarify the business, audience, goals, content, constraints, and intended outcome.
2. **Design** — establish the visual system, structure, experience, and interaction direction.
3. **Build** — implement, integrate, test, and refine the approved solution.
4. **Launch and Care** — deploy, configure domains and external services, verify the live result, and establish ongoing support when selected.

### 7.7 About

Describe Northline as a design-led digital studio with boutique attention and production-grade execution. Preserve the value of direct collaboration, but do not limit the studio identity to website design or emphasize a single maker in a way that suggests limited capability.

### 7.8 FAQ

Update the FAQ to cover:

- the types of work Northline takes on;
- whether Northline can improve an existing website, store, or system;
- how project scope and timing are determined;
- what happens after launch;
- the separation between Northline fees and third-party platform costs.

### 7.9 Contact

Keep contact as a static email display with no form and no `mailto:` link. Invite the client to describe:

- what they are building or operating;
- the current website, store, or system, if one exists;
- what is not working or what they want to improve;
- whether they need a new build, an improvement, or ongoing management;
- relevant timing, goals, and context.

An early idea remains sufficient to start a conversation. The contact section should continue displaying `northlinestudio425@gmail.com` until a branded mailbox is configured in a separate project.

### 7.10 Footer

Update the footer summary to cover websites, commerce, business systems, and ongoing care. Keep the brand, essential section links, copyright, and back-to-top behavior.

## 8. Implementation Architecture

This remains a static, single-page Next.js/vinext site. The change does not add a backend, database, form handler, authentication, or new product dependency.

Expected implementation work:

- update service, pricing, FAQ, and section copy in `app/page.tsx`;
- replace the five service records with four approved service-family records;
- reshape the introductory section into the three engagement paths;
- update pricing cards and support panels to match the approved model;
- make only the CSS changes required for the new content and responsive layout in `app/globals.css`;
- preserve the existing SVG logo, font treatment, fixed header, section-link scrolling behavior, and mobile-menu interaction;
- update metadata in `app/layout.tsx` where the existing website-only phrasing conflicts with the new positioning;
- update rendered-HTML tests to enforce the new content structure and guard against regressions.

The current page can continue using local arrays for static service, pricing, and FAQ content. No content-management layer is justified for this revision.

## 9. Interaction, Failure, and Accessibility Requirements

- All header, mobile-menu, CTA, footer, and back-to-top links must continue reaching valid section IDs.
- Section navigation must not lock document scrolling after a jump.
- The mobile menu must close after navigation and continue responding to outside click and Escape.
- The page must remain usable if JavaScript-enhanced scrolling is unavailable; hash destinations provide the fallback.
- Contact must remain usable without client-side form logic by displaying a copyable email address and inquiry guidance.
- Heading order, landmarks, navigation labels, logo accessible name, focus behavior, contrast, and responsive text sizing must remain valid.
- No upper-right arrow glyphs should be reintroduced into buttons or links.

Because this is static content, there is no new runtime error state. Third-party platform availability, client accounts, credentials, and integration failures belong to future client projects, not this marketing-site revision.
